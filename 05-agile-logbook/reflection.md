# 5. Agile Logbook — Personal Reflection (STAR)

[← Back to Second Assessment](../index.md#agile-assessment)



## Situation

Our project required regular progress tracking and early identification of obstacles.

## Task

I needed to document what was completed, what would happen next and any blockers.

## Action

The supplied logbook contains draft stand-up entries for 12–16 October, noting authentication, booking, offers, testing and deployment risks. I have kept them labelled as drafts because the document explicitly requests confirmation against actual work.

## Result

The logbook provides a structure for reviewing progress, but not yet verified proof of daily activity. I learned to capture dated evidence during each sprint rather than reconstructing it later.
