# 3. Product Backlog — Personal Reflection (STAR)

[← Back to Second Assessment](../index.md#agile-assessment)



## Situation

We needed a way to organise all required features without losing track of unfinished work.

## Task

My task was to help keep development requirements visible and prioritised.

## Action

The supplied taskboard places US01–US12 under Product Backlog, leaving In Progress and Completed empty. It also records possible bottlenecks such as integration and deployment configuration.

## Result

The backlog gives the team a shared overview of intended work. I learned that transparent task status is more useful than marking tasks completed without supporting evidence.
