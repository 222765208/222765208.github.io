# 1. Agile Team Charter — Personal Reflection (STAR)

[← Back to Second Assessment](../index.md#agile-assessment)



## Situation

Our three-member team needed a shared understanding of responsibilities while developing Shezz-App.

## Task

I needed to clarify how my software-development responsibilities would connect with Vumani's frontend work and Aliziwe's UI/UX work.

## Action

The charter documented our proposed roles, WhatsApp communication, Trello tracking, GitHub collaboration, decision-making, Definition of Done and project risks. My reported work included integration, testing and deployment; the charter itself does not prove that all planned responsibilities were completed.

## Result

We have a written framework for coordinating the project and reviewing workload. I learned that an agreed charter should be checked against actual contributions and updated when responsibilities change. The team commitment signatures remain unconfirmed.
