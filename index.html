Professional Reflections

What I learned through building, presenting and publishing my portfolio.

1. Creating My CV in Markdown

Situation: I needed to create and present my CV using Markdown on GitHub.

Task: Structure the information so it was clean, professional and easy to read.

Action: I practised headings, lists and spacing, previewed changes, and adjusted the formatting until it was consistent.

Result: I produced a more organised CV and became more confident using Markdown to document my work.

2. Mock Interview Experience

Situation: I recorded a mock interview to practise presenting myself professionally.

Task: Answer questions clearly and demonstrate confidence and preparation.

Action: I practised my answers, focused on speaking clearly, and paid attention to tone and body language.

Result: The experience helped me recognise strengths and areas to improve, including reducing filler words and making answers more direct.

3. Publishing with GitHub Pages

Situation: I wanted my portfolio to be accessible online.

Task: Publish the website and check that updates appeared correctly.

Action: I configured GitHub Pages, committed updates to the repository, and checked the deployed site.

Result: I gained practical experience with version control and static-site deployment, and learned to verify changes after publishing.

4. CAPACITI WIL and Agile Collaboration

Situation: During my WIL at CAPACITI, I contributed to the Shezz-App workplace project.

Task: Work with the team and document planning artefacts and progress for the Agile assessment.

Action: I used the Trello board and available team communication records to organise user stories, tasks and sprint-related evidence.

Result: I strengthened my understanding of Agile planning and the importance of using evidence to explain contributions and learning.
