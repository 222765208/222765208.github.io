## Connecting the Evidence to My WIL Learning

Across the five areas, the evidence demonstrates how team agreements, user stories, prioritised backlogs, sprint tasks and communication fit together. The charter establishes expectations; user stories explain the user need; the product backlog orders possible work; the sprint backlog turns selected work into an actionable plan; and the logbook supports reflection and follow-up.

My key learning from my CAPACITI WIL experience is that technical delivery depends not only on building features, but also on clear communication, planning, evidence and continuous improvement. I will apply this by writing clearer tasks, recording progress honestly, raising blockers early and linking evidence to the learning outcome it demonstrates.
